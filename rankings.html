Reading a row

A keeper's Sum is the whole rating. Her four Rk columns show where it came from. The strongest profiles have no weak factor: the top three keepers have no rank worse than 45th on any factor. Keepers who dominate one factor and collapse on another land in the middle. The method rewards keepers who hold up on all four factors over keepers who are extreme on one.
